About BIS

The Bureau of Indian Standards

The BIS is the National Standards Body of India, established under the BIS Act, 2016. It is responsible for the harmonious development of standardization activities in India — covering the establishment of standards, accreditation, and quality certification.

BIS publishes over 19,000+ Indian Standards (IS) across diverse sectors including electrical equipment, cement, food products, textiles, automobile parts, and construction materials. It also implements the Quality Control Orders (QCOs) — mandatory certification requirements for 110+ products across 24 ministries.

For precious metals, BIS operates the Hallmarking & Uniquely Identifying Number (HUID) scheme under IS 2112, ensuring gold purity verification through a nationwide network of 350+ assaying & hallmarking centres.

Standards Lookup Intent: STANDARD_LOOKUP · Confidence: 0.97
Prompt

What is the IS standard for electrical plugs?

Response

The relevant standard is IS 1293:2019 — "Plugs and socket-outlets for domestic and similar purposes."

Rated voltage up to 250V, rated current up to 16A. Available under Scheme I (ISI Mark) under the technical committee ETD 14 (Electrical Accessories).

QCO Crosswalk Intent: CROSSWALK_LOOKUP · Confidence: 0.94
Prompt

Which QCO applies to steel rods and bars?

Response

QCO R.O. 123(E) applies to steel rods and bars (HS code 7214, 7215, 7216). It falls under the DPIIT notification dated 2021, mandating ISI certification under Scheme I.

The product requires conformity assessment through a Schedule-2 testing laboratory and third-party inspection.
